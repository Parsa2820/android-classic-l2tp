Local changes on top of that commit:
- JNI class names point at dev.l2tpipsec.client (VpnBridge, VpnTunnelEvents, TunnelVpnService).
- tunnel_disconnect() and the nativeDisconnect JNI entry close a negotiated session.
- ipsec_negotiate() keeps esp_fd at -1 until a live socket exists, and clears it after a failed handshake so a later close cannot hit stdin or a recycled descriptor.
- C standard is C11. mbedTLS is the vendored 2.28.9 tree with MBEDTLS_MD4_C enabled for MS-CHAPv2.
